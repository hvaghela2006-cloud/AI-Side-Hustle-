There's a starting point built for you

The guide includes a framework for choosing your first hustle. Here's the short version.

Need income sooner?

Start with a service-based opportunity — freelance writing, chatbot building, or VA services.

Want a long-term asset?

Explore digital products or content assets, like Notion templates or a faceless YouTube channel.

Have zero budget?

Look at options with little or no startup cost, like data annotation or freelance writing.

Technically curious?

Try vibe coding, workflow automation, or chatbot building for local businesses.

Prefer creative work?

Look at AI image generation, print-on-demand design, or real estate photo editing.
